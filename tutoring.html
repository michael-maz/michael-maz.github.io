Whether you’re looking for your first role or moving from another field, I can help you understand what the work involves and practise the skills you’ll use on the job.

Investment Management

Pick a company, look at how it makes money and practise explaining what would make it an attractive investment.

Project Management

Use a project from work, university or volunteering to practise setting deadlines, dividing up the work and deciding what to do when plans change.

Business Analysis

Take a process you know, map how it works today and practise turning problems into clear requirements for a better version.
