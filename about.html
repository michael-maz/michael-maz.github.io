I’m Michael, a business analyst and career tutor. I help students and people changing careers understand what different roles involve and practise the skills they’ll use at work.

What I Bring to Tutoring

At Nationwide, I brought together the business requirements for two sets of regulatory changes. That meant turning complex rules into clear requirements that teams could work from. I’ve also worked on requirements and test design for Oracle and SAP systems.

In a business analysis session, we can practise that same skill on a process you know from work, university or volunteering.

I’ve helped over 1,000 people through my career advice on TikTok. Away from work, I enjoy travelling and horse riding.
