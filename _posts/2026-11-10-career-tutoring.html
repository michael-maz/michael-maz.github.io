I’m now offering tutoring for students and career changers who want to get into investment management, project management or business analysis. Whether you’re applying for your first role or moving from another field, we can look at what the work involves, which skills you already have and what you need to practise next.

Investment Management

Learn how investment teams research companies and assess opportunities. We can pick a company from an industry you’ve worked in or a business you’re curious about, look at how it makes money and practise explaining what would make you want to invest in it.

Project Management

Learn how to turn an idea into a plan and keep the work on track. We can take something you’ve already helped deliver, from a university society event to a change at work, and use it to practise setting deadlines, dividing up the work and deciding what to do when plans change.

Business Analysis

Learn how to ask better questions about a business problem and turn the answers into clear requirements. Think of a process you’ve found frustrating at work, in a part-time job or while volunteering. We can map how it works today and work out what a better version would need to do.

Get in Touch

If you’re interested, send me a message with a little about what you study or do now, which area you’re exploring and what you’d like help with. If you’re deciding between a couple of paths, tell me what interests you about each.
